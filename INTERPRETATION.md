# ShopPulse — Sample Data Interpretation

**Dataset:** 180 fictional transactions from January–June 2026. These are illustrative figures, not real business records.

- **Total sales revenue:** PHP 82,180.00. This measures sales value, not profit; expenses and costs are not tracked.
- **Average transaction value:** PHP 456.56.
- **Highest-revenue category:** Accessories, contributing PHP 76,380.00 (92.9% of revenue).
- **Strongest month:** 2026-03, with PHP 17,493.00 revenue.
- **Top item by revenue:** Headset (PHP 28,290.00).

## Recommendations
Review inventory and promotion for the highest-revenue items; compare monthly trends before changing prices; collect cost data to evaluate profit, not just revenue.

## Limitations
This is synthetic data for a classroom demonstration. It cannot establish actual demand, customer behavior, or profitability.
